# Practitioner interview script

Time: 30 minutes. Record only with consent. Take quotes in the person's words.

## Rules

1. Do not pitch Intra before question 6.
2. Do not lead with Intra's wedge.
3. Ask for the last real incident, not opinions.

## Script

1. What is your role? Who owns production access decisions?
2. Walk me through the last incident that needed production data. What did you open first?
3. Where did access block you? How long did the delay last?
4. Who can reach production today, and how? Ask for the full path: VPN, bastion, cloud console, database tool, SaaS admin.
5. Has your team ever cut broad access after a scare? What work did that cut break?
6. If a read-only, timed, logged path had existed that day, would it have changed the incident? Why?
7. Who approves emergency access when the owner is away? What happens if nobody answers?
8. Do humans and robots or agents share one access policy today? Where does that hurt?
9. Which tools already cover this? What do they miss for you?
10. May I watch one real access workflow this week? May I see a sanitized access map?

## Close

- Ask for one introduction to another tech lead or infra owner.
- Record consent for a follow-up lab walkthrough.
