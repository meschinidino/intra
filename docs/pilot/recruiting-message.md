# Recruiting message

Target mix: at least three tech leads or infra owners. At most two agent builders.
Channels: former colleagues, local developer and infra communities, open-source
maintainers who run production, small-team or MSP contacts.

## Short message

Hi [name]. I am researching how small teams handle production access during
incidents. I need 30 minutes, not a sales call. I will ask about your last
incident, your current access path, and where access blocked you. I record
notes only with your consent. No product pitch. Can we talk this week?

## Follow-up if no reply in five days

Hi [name]. One follow-up only. The call stays 30 minutes. Your incident
experience is the evidence I need, even if your current setup works well.
Does [two concrete times] work?

## Log every attempt

Record channel, date, reply, and booked time in the
[evidence tracker](evidence-tracker.md). Fewer than five booked calls at week
end is itself evidence. Report the count and channels tried.
