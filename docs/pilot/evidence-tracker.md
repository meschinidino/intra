## Gate check (from the governing design)

- [ ] Five interviews done.
- [ ] At least three of five describe flat reach, a blunt cut, or blocked incident reads.
- [ ] At least two of those are tech leads or infra owners.
- [ ] At least two agreed to a lab walkthrough or a sanitized access map.
- [ ] Kill check: split human/robot policy does not outrank blocked incident access.
- [ ] Kill check: incumbents do not already satisfy the wedge.
