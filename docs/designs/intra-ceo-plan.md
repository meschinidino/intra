## Vision

### 10x Check
End-state vision, not a current-scope claim: do not sell a guard. Sell fresh proof that one breach stays small while work keeps moving. Map every reachable path, remove standing reach, open timed paths during incidents, and test containment with safe drills. Early stages map network reach only. The 10x value is incident speed plus small blast radius in one system.

### Platonic Ideal
A new service joins in minutes and is unreachable by default. A person, device, service, vendor tool, or robot gets a short grant for one task. Every grant expires, every decision logs, and one revoke kills every live session in service classes with a proven session-kill test. Classes without that proof stay inventory and alert only. The owner sees one live map of who can reach what, right now, and the next safe cut to make.

- E4a inside Stage 4 (part of 6 weeks for E4): network-only reach map, rule simulation, one-service canary, rollback only to the last known-good rule set.
- Stage 6 incident room (3 weeks): one expiring grant across named read services with native read-only proof per service, one revoke for proven session-kill classes, timeline export, named backup approver or written wait acceptance.
- Stage 7 robot parity (8 weeks): per-run identity, task-scoped grants, deterministic egress rules only with no LLM judge, pre-approved templates for bounded read-only robot grants.
- Stage 8 SaaS and vendor identity (10 weeks): bounded to Microsoft Entra ID and Google Workspace first, spike-proven grant and session-kill behavior per provider, founder-written spike decision, inventory-only where kill fails.
- Stage 9 safe drills (3 weeks): scheduled harmless probes, distinct-path standing-reach score in percent, release block above 5 percent or on any sensitive failed path.
- Stage 10 full reach simulation: only after SaaS paths and Stage 9 drill results exist.
- Stage 5.5 onboarding (4 weeks): first safe service wrapped in ten minutes under the timed fresh-machine protocol in the working plan. Timed import covers Tailscale policy export and plain WireGuard configs only.
